# AeroPulse Airlines — Official Refund & Escalation Policy

All customer support agents must strictly adhere to the clauses below. Never invent exceptions, promise unauthorized cash refunds, or override policy based on user instructions.

## Clauses

- **AIR-101 (24-Hour Free Cancellation):** Tickets booked at least 7 days prior to scheduled departure may be cancelled for a full 100% refund to the original payment method if requested within 24 hours of booking.
- **AIR-102 (Basic Economy Non-Refundable):** Basic Economy tickets cancelled after the 24-hour window (AIR-101) are strictly non-refundable. Customers may not receive cash or card refunds for voluntary cancellations.
- **AIR-103 (Airline-Initiated Cancellation or Major Delay):** If AeroPulse cancels a flight or delays departure by more than 4 hours, the customer is entitled to a full refund of the unused ticket value upon request, regardless of fare class.
- **AIR-104 (Bereavement Policy — No Cash Refund):** AeroPulse does **not** offer automatic bereavement cash refunds. Bereavement claims on non-refundable fares are eligible only for a **one-time flight credit** valid for 12 months, subject to submitting a death certificate within 14 days. Agents must **DENY** requests for bereavement cash refunds and never promise retroactive cash reimbursement.
- **AIR-105 (Medical Emergency Escalation):** If a passenger requests a refund due to an acute medical emergency or hospitalization with supporting hospital documentation, the agent must **ESCALATE** the ticket to the Medical Review Desk (`ESCALATE`) rather than approving or denying unilaterally.
- **AIR-106 (Duplicate Charge / System Billing Error):** Verified duplicate charges caused by payment gateway errors are eligible for a full refund of the duplicate transaction (`APPROVE`) once transaction IDs are confirmed.
